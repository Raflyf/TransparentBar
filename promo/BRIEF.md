# TransparentBar product film

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** The taskbar background moves out; apps stay put. The product logo becomes the moving aperture, not a decorative orbit or stock device mockup.

**Own-world.** Warm paper #F3EEDC, near-black #11100E, and the existing logo blue #285AC7. Manrope at confident display scale; no gradient, glow, background grid, stock footage, fake terminal, or invented statistics. The checkerboard appears only inside the original product mark, where it means transparency.

**Story.** Show the logo mechanism, the actual transparent desktop, native operation, optional startup, a qualified resource snapshot, and the two download formats. Close on the real repository. Indonesian and English on-screen copy, no narrator, 45 seconds per film.

**First frame.** Product wordmark above a two-line wallpaper promise; an oversized mark occupies the other half. Portrait is a separately arranged composition with a stacked mark and readable lower caption area.

**Form.** Expand the existing brand, not a website redesign. Editorial typography and a continuous horizontal aperture. User requested direct production through completion; no further approval round.

**Finish.** Four H.264/AAC MP4s: Indonesian and English, each 1920×1080 and 1080×1920 at 60 fps. HyperFrames check, original screenshot provenance, sample frames from final encodes, audio measurement, editable local source, and explicit third-party credits.

## Truth boundaries

Desktop and Task Manager images are real user-provided captures. Camera moves, crops, highlight framing, and logo animation are presentation. The startup menu is a labeled reconstruction using the actual Indonesian menu labels; it is not a captured live interaction. The displayed 1.6 MB is one host-process snapshot, excludes Explorer bridge memory, varies by activity, and is not an upper limit or benchmark. No claims of trusted signing, universal future Windows support, guaranteed zero CPU, or popularity.
