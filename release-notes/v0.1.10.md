## TransparentBar v0.1.10

Makes the connector react to scrolling sooner, fades between edge colours instead of snapping, and trims the strip slightly.

### Changed
- Active sampling interval is now 60 ms (was 120 ms), so the strip tracks scrolling roughly twice as fast.
- Sampled rows are weighted toward the row nearest the window edge. Newly scrolled content therefore registers as soon as it appears at the edge, instead of waiting for it to fill the whole four-row band first.
- Colours now cross-fade: each frame closes 30% of the remaining gap per column, so a colour change eases in over a few frames instead of switching abruptly. When the strip is hidden or resized the fade restarts cleanly rather than interpolating from a stale edge.
- Strip height at 96 DPI drops from 42 px to 34 px, which makes the connector a little less prominent while keeping the same falloff profile.

### Verified
- Native tests pass: 17 host tests and 7 bridge tests. New tests assert that the fade converges over multiple frames instead of snapping, and that the edge-most sampled row dominates the result while a brighter far row cannot win.
- `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`, and the release build all pass locally.

### Unchanged
Per-monitor DPI awareness, DPI-scaled geometry, per-column colour sampling, the 1000 ms idle eligibility probe, fullscreen suppression, the tray toggle, English UI, system-themed menu, update dialogs, startup settings, and taskbar restoration.

### Limitations
- Colours remain a smoothed approximation of a thin visible screen edge; text, tooltips, protected content, HDR, and display colour processing can still affect the sample.
- The fade is a short ease, not a scroll-linked animation; it cannot make the strip move in perfect lockstep with a fast flick.
- Connector support is still limited to the primary bottom-docked taskbar with auto-hide off.
- Native tests do not prove visual behaviour on every monitor layout. Binaries remain unsigned.
